Scramble Eggs

Eggs are a versatile ingredient that can be prepared in many ways.

Ingredients:

  • Eggs
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Butter or oil

Method:

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk them together.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and add butter or oil.
  3. Pour the eggs into the pan and let them cook for a few seconds.
  4. Gently stir the eggs with a spatula until they are cooked to your desired consistency.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
